Isometric Piping

Start the route

Touch anywhere to put the first point down, then keep clicking where the pipe goes — it snaps to the six isometric directions and carries on from each point.

Tap on open sheet or press Esc to stop. To carry on from a point, tap it and use Draw from here. Or use the Command tab and type N 1500.

With a pencil: the pencil draws, fingers move the sheet, two fingers zoom.
